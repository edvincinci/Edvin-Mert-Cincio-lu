PROJO

Smart Home Entertainment Robot
2024 October — January
TypeSmart Device
TechAI + Projection
TargetFamilies

A friendly, AI-powered home robot designed to enhance quality time at home — transforming the home's atmosphere through light, sound, and projection. Hover over the image to browse renderings.

Vision
The vision of this project is to enhance the quality of time spent at home by offering activities for family members and enriching individual experiences. This robot, designed to be fun and friendly, will transform the home's atmosphere and provide unforgettable experiences.
Target User
Designed for anyone who wants to bring new energy and companionship into their home. With its nature and character, PROJO encourages household members to come together and enjoy shared moments.
Key Features
  • Proximity sensors + room mapping
  • Built-in projector (table, wall, ceiling)
  • AI-powered conversation and task management
  • 360° wheels for navigation
  • Movable neck with 5 emotional expressions
  • LED screen for visual communication
  • Returns to charging station when idle
Operating Modes
  • Movie Experience — light games, sound effects, projection
  • Ambiance Changer — music + projection
  • Party Mode — lights, music, games
  • Study Assistant — web search, projection, white noise
Charging Station
~5 hours overnight charge. Returns automatically when idle. Remains responsive even while charging.

Travel · Portable · Ergonomic Design

UGMA

Portable Garment Steamer for Travelers
2025 January — May
TypeConsumer Product
MethodShadowing + Interview
FocusErgonomics

A minimalist portable garment steamer for globally active professionals. Three critical problems — water-filling difficulty, poor storage, ergonomic issues — identified through shadowing and user interviews, then solved by design.

Research Method
Deep user research through shadowing and interviews with a 45-year-old business professional who travels frequently for work. Three core pain points emerged:
  • Water Filling — Hard to fill, not practical
  • Storage — Too big for shelves, cable management issues
  • Ergonomics — Interface problems, wrong indicator lights
Target User
A globally active professional with an intense schedule, frequently traveling abroad. Needs efficiency, portability, and comfort within a fast-paced mobile lifestyle.
Design Solutions
  • Wide-nozzle tank for easy filling at any tap
  • Foldable 180° rotating head for compact storage
  • U-shaped body prevents hand contact with hot surfaces
  • Clear LED indicators (ready / heating / wait)
  • 13 precisely engineered components
  • Matt PVC + stainless steel plate + ceramic cooler
User Scenario
Take from bag → open wide water tank → fill → plug in → rotate head 180° → steam clothing → rotate head back → return to bag.

MODEL
MARKET

Student Material Marketplace
2025
TypeMobile App
Users1016+ Students
Savings3.6M TL/yr

A student-powered marketplace for buying, selling, and renting leftover architectural materials at Özyeğin University. Backed by survey data from 100 students. 68.2% always have leftover materials. Saves ~890 TL per project.

The Problem
Architecture and design students waste expensive materials every semester. An example architectural model costs over 1,925 TL from the university shop. With ModelMarket the same project costs 1,035 TL — saving 890 TL per project, per student.
Survey Results (100 Students)
  • 68.2% have leftovers after every project
  • 34% spend 1,000–2,000 TL monthly on materials
  • 45.5% buy from the university frequently
  • #1 problem: materials too expensive
Key Features
  • Campus-only access via school email
  • Rent or sell with photos and descriptions
  • Seller ratings and feedback mechanism
  • 24/7 access (shop closes at 17:30)
  • Damage insurance on rented items
  • In-app messaging for campus meetup
  • Premium membership with promoted listings
Competitive Advantage
  • Campus-specific vs Letgo / Sahibinden
  • Renting mechanism (unique in market)
  • Architectural materials specialized
Revenue Model
  • Commission on each successful transaction
  • Premium membership (ad-free + promoted listings)
  • Banner & interstitial ads for free users
SWOT Highlights
  • Strength: Budget-friendly, reduces waste, on-campus access
  • Opportunity: Expansion to other universities, brand partnerships
  • Weakness: Unknown quality of materials, damage risk
  • Threat: University policy restrictions

Safety · AR · Hotel Emergency

AERO-SHELL

Advanced Safety Mask
2025–2026 · Graduation Studio
TypeWearable Safety
TechAR + HEPA / Carbon
TargetHotel Guests

A smart mask system that guides hotel guests through smoke during emergencies and protects them from inhalation, with wall mask stations and a rescue command center for responders.

Problem
85% of building-fire deaths are caused by smoke inhalation. Smoke cuts visibility by up to 90%, panic erodes decision-making, and the escape window is only a few golden minutes.
Selected SDG
SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities and Communities: safe, inclusive and resilient hospitality infrastructure, with personal evacuation technology as a new standard for hotels.
Global Evidence
Station Hotel (USA, 2003), Grenfell Tower (UK, 2017), Kemerovo Mall (Russia, 2018) and Kartalkaya Hotel (Türkiye, 2025).

Key Features
  • AR navigation — green arrows reflected in the visor
  • HEPA + carbon smoke filtration
  • Transparent anti-fog visor, no loss of vision
  • Hotel integration with the fire system via BLE
  • Head lamp and adjustable strap
Mask Stations
  • Translucent cover and fireproof body
  • Seal prevents unnecessary use
  • Flashing green light and sound alert in emergencies
  • Main dock (adult) and child dock
  • Configurable for twin, family and hostel rooms
Rescue Command Center

Sustainable Outdoor Water Boiler
2025–2026
TypeProduct Family
TechInduction + Harvested Energy
TargetTrekkers / Campers

An outdoor induction water boiler powered by solar, kinetic and wind or water energy, replacing campfires and gas stoves with a portable, low-impact system that fits in one case.

Research
Campfires damage soil, degrade air quality and disrupt wildlife. Gas stoves used in trekking can produce 40% more carbon emissions than alternative methods.
Target Users
Outdoor enthusiasts, campers and homeowners who want convenient hot water. Key needs: safety, lightweight, portable, easy setup and fast boiling.
Concept
Fast boiling, easy communication and energy efficiency, shifting outdoor hot drinks from a necessity to a pleasure-driven experience.
